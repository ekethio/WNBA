Quarter Tendencies

—
How to read this: Each game, a team's number in one period is compared with another period of the same game, then the league's usual difference is subtracted (e.g. every team plays slower in Q4). What's left is the team's tendency. Margin: each quarter vs the team's other three, and 1st half vs 2nd half. Total: Q1 vs Q2 and Q3 vs Q4 only, so both quarters are played toward the same baskets; half totals aren't compared because they depend on which end a team chooses to shoot at first. Pace (possessions per 40 min): each quarter vs the other three. Over % counts games above the league-average total for that period, not a betting line. Halves exclude overtime.
